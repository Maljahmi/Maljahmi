# Mohamed Aljahmi

Undergraduate in Political Science and Sociology at Queens College, City University of New York (B.A. expected May 2027), with minors in Data Analytics and Legal Studies.

### Research

My research focuses on race, ethnicity, and politics, with particular attention to how Middle Eastern and North African (MENA) and Muslim Americans are measured, categorized, and often rendered invisible in federal data and survey research, and I approach racialization as a measurement problem using quantitative methods in R.
